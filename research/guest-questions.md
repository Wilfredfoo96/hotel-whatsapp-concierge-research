# Harper Boutique Hotel — Guest Demand List (WhatsApp)

**What this is:** the realistic inventory of what guests actually message the hotel's WhatsApp number, in their own words, ranked by how often it comes up. Written from the guest side, not the system side. 89 distinct items.

**Property facts this list is grounded in** (from harper-hotel.com and OTA listings, Oct 2026):

- 45 rooms, **9 room categories**, boutique. Front desk is on the **4th Floor, Block A, Sutera Avenue** — a mixed commercial development in Sembulan, Kota Kinabalu, not a standalone hotel building. 24/7 front desk.
- Address: A-3A-16, Lot 16 & 17, Block A Sutera Avenue, Lorong Lebuh Sutera, Sembulan, 88100 Kota Kinabalu, Sabah. Hotline +6016 880 3889, stay@harper-hotel.com, booking engine on secure.guestpro.net.
- **No breakfast** unless stated. **No pool, no gym, no on-site restaurant** on the hotel's own facilities page. IMAGO mall ~5 min walk; ~10 min by car to BKI airport.
- Check-in 15:00 / check-out 12:00. **Early check-in RM80 nett**, subject to availability. **Late checkout: 2–3pm RM50, 4–5pm RM80, 6pm onward = one night's rate.**
- Free cancellation ≥3 days before arrival; inside 3 days or no-show = **full stay charged**. Online refunds 7–14 working days.
- **RM100 security deposit per booking, in cash**, refunded at checkout after room inspection.
- Payment: credit/debit card, bank online transfer, QR payment, e-wallet, **ePerolehan** (= government/agency guests exist).
- **Tourism tax RM10 per room per night, foreign nationals only.** Malaysians (MyKad) and PRs exempt. Payable at check-in.
- Valid photo ID (IC or passport) mandatory at check-in. **Under-18s cannot book**; a guest under 18 needs parental permission given at check-in.
- **Strictly non-smoking, vape and e-cigarettes included — RM500 cleaning fee if detected.** No pets. Lost/damaged key card RM50.
- **Durian, mangosteen and jackfruit are banned on the premises — RM300 penalty.**
- 2 of the 9 room types are explicitly **windowless** (Standard Queen 11m², Deluxe Double Queen 22m²). Deluxe Double Queen is the wheelchair-friendly room; lift to all floors; shower grab bars.
- In-room: smart TV with streaming, kettle + instant coffee/tea, bottled water, towels, slippers, toothbrush, shower cap, **iron and ironing board**, hangers.
- Parking is **not the hotel's own**: Sutera Avenue basement car park RM2.50 first 2 hours then RM1.50/hour (from 1 Jul 2025); Harbour City open-air lot opposite, free first 15 min. OTA listings contradict this with "MYR 1/hour" and "MYR 24/day".
- OTA listings state **cots and extra beds cannot be added**, and that guests must **contact the property a day in advance with their arrival time**.
- Staff languages: English, Malay, Mandarin ("Chinese-speaking staff on duty from time to time").
- Top complaint in 288 reviews (8.9/10): **noise** — road, nightclub and rooftop. Then small rooms, then linen/towel freshness.

**Evidence base for frequency:** HiJiffy's Top 50 Questions report is the only channel-split data I found — its **WhatsApp top three are check-in, parking, and managing a booking** (website chat: check-in, meal plans, parking). WeSpeak and myhotel.cl independently put availability, rate, cancellation, parking, pets, breakfast, check-in/out times, wifi and children's options at the top. Malaysian-specific items are grounded where possible (tourism tax, Muslim-friendly amenity norms from malaysia.gov.my, LHDN e-invoice buyer fields, KK Grab fares and Jesselton Point ferry patterns). Rows I could not source are tagged **(inf.)** — inference from this property's own policies plus general Malaysian hotel practice.

## Top 10 by damage if unanswered

Ranked by what it actually costs — a lost booking, a 1-star review, or a chargeback — not by how often it arrives.

1. **"I'm at Sutera Avenue and can't find the hotel"** (#42, #86) — reception on the 4th floor of a multi-block commercial development, at night, guest in a car park. Angry guest *and* bad review, every time. The highest-damage item on this list and the most property-specific.
2. **OTA booking the hotel can't find, guest already in the lobby** (#81) — lost revenue, a review, and possibly an OTA penalty. Must never be answered confidently without the extranet.
3. **Breakfast assumed included** (#3, #63) — the gap between an OTA listing and reality is a documented review-killer, and it is discovered at 8am when nothing can be fixed.
4. **Extra bed / baby cot** (#9, #10) — the true answer is *no*. Any softer answer hands a family an unusable room on arrival.
5. **Aircon or water heater dead at night** (#54, #55) — a 2am problem in KK heat with no human on the other end is the fastest path to a 1-star review.
6. **Noise, with no room move offered** (#56) — already the top complaint in 288 reviews. Unanswered, it is a guaranteed review.
7. **Double charge / deposit not returned** (#82, #78) — escalates past the hotel into a card chargeback, where the hotel loses by default.
8. **Early check-in and late checkout quoted as free** (#6, #7, #44, #74) — RM80 / RM50 / full-night surprises at the desk. Policy is correct; the damage is entirely in how it was promised on WhatsApp.
9. **Cancellation inside 3 days, or a no-show, billed in full** (#33, #84) — correct policy, maximum anger, and the guest's only outlet is a public review.
10. **Company invoice and LHDN e-invoice** (#38, #73, #76) — gets no review, but a business traveller who can't claim the stay never books again, and neither does their company. Quiet, compounding revenue loss.

Runner-up: **tourism tax RM10 sprung on a foreign guest at check-in** (#48) — small money, pure surprise, disproportionate irritation.

## Five a hotel bot is most likely to have overlooked

1. **"Which block, which lift, which floor?"** — not "where is the hotel". Reception is **4th Floor, Block A** inside Sutera Avenue, a mixed commercial development. Every bot answers location with a Google Maps pin and a 5-minute-walk-to-IMAGO line; the pin drops the guest at the wrong lobby. This single question is the property's top damage item and the one a generic FAQ cannot possibly contain.
2. **The durian / mangosteen / jackfruit ban — RM300.** Published in the T&Cs, in Sabah, in fruit season, and in a guest's hands when they walk in. No generic hotel FAQ has a fruit policy.
3. **"Cots and extra beds cannot be added."** Every hotel bot in the world answers extra beds with "subject to availability, surcharge applies". Here that is false, and the cost lands on a family that has already arrived.
4. **ePerolehan** — the published payment list includes Malaysia's government procurement system, which means government and GLC guests who need a quotation, a Local Order number and an invoice addressed to an agency before anyone travels. A bot set up for cards and e-wallets will not recognise the request, and this is high-value repeat business.
5. **The parking answer is contradictory and not the hotel's to give.** The hotel's own page says RM2.50 for the first 2 hours then RM1.50/hour at the Sutera Avenue basement; OTA listings say RM1/hour and RM24/day; there is also a free-first-15-minutes open lot opposite. A bot will confidently quote whichever number it ingested, on the #2 most-asked WhatsApp topic.

Also worth checking: the OTA-imposed obligation to **notify arrival time a day in advance** (the hotel's own site never mentions it, yet it generates traffic and sets a guest expectation of a reply); the **two windowless room types**, which need flagging before booking rather than explaining after; and **under-18s cannot book at all**, with parental permission required at check-in — an auto-cancellation risk nobody asks about until it fires.
